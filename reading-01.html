ABOUT THIS READING

Understanding the consultation

This consultation looks at the hand as a complete system rather than isolating one line and treating it as a prediction. The reading considers the major lines, mounts, fingers, hand shape, markings and the balance between different features. Particular attention is given to patterns that repeat across the hand and to areas that appear unusually strong, developed or marked.

The reading can explore personality, emotional nature, relationships, career tendencies, personal strengths and recurring challenges. It is especially useful when you want a visual, pattern-based perspective on yourself and your current direction. The interpretation is grounded in what is actually visible in the hands provided, rather than forcing a predetermined narrative onto the reading.

WHAT WE EXPLORE
  • What the hands reveal
  • Major lines and their patterns
  • Mounts, fingers and hand structure
  • Relationships and emotional patterns
  • Career, direction and recurring themes
BEFORE YOUR READING

For the clearest reading, provide clear photographs of both palms, with the full hand visible and enough detail to distinguish the lines. Additional images of the side of the hand and fingers can be useful when a particular feature needs closer examination.
