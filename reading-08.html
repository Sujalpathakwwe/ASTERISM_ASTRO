Life Path Deep Reading

A broad consultation designed to connect the major areas of life into one coherent picture rather than concentrating on a single question.

ABOUT THIS READING

Understanding the consultation

The Life Path Deep Reading is the broadest format. It looks across several areas of the chart to understand how personality, relationships, family, career, finances and personal development interact with one another.

Instead of producing disconnected observations about individual houses, the consultation tries to build a coherent narrative from the chart. It can be useful when you feel that several areas of life are changing at once or when you want a deeper understanding of your overall direction rather than an answer to one isolated question.

WHAT WE EXPLORE
  • Personality and emotional patterns
  • Relationships and family themes
  • Education and career
  • Finances and life direction
  • Major turning points and development
BEFORE YOUR READING

Prepare your birth details and a short note about the areas of life you most want included. This allows the broader reading to remain relevant rather than becoming unnecessarily generic.
