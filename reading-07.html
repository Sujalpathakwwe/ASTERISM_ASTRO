Relationship Deep Dive

A comprehensive relationship consultation for complex, long-standing or emotionally significant connections that require more than a short compatibility reading.

ABOUT THIS READING

Understanding the consultation

This consultation examines both individual charts and the relationship created between them. It explores emotional needs, attraction, communication, conflict, expectations, commitment and the ways each person may interpret the relationship differently.

Because the reading is comprehensive, it can address several connected questions rather than isolating one issue. It is particularly useful when a relationship has a long history, repeated cycles, distance, uncertainty or major decisions attached to it. The focus is on explaining the dynamics and their context rather than giving a simplistic verdict.

WHAT WE EXPLORE
  • Individual relationship patterns
  • Synastry and emotional dynamics
  • Communication and conflict
  • Commitment and long-term potential
  • Timing and major relationship periods
BEFORE YOUR READING

Accurate birth details for both people are essential. A concise timeline of the relationship and the main question you want explored can make the consultation more focused.
