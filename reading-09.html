Dasha & Timing Reading

A specialised timing consultation centred on planetary periods and the themes that become more prominent during particular phases of life.

ABOUT THIS READING

Understanding the consultation

Timing is considered by looking at the active planetary periods in relation to the natal chart. The reading examines the planets involved in the Mahadasha and Antardasha, their placement and house ownership, and the way current or upcoming transits interact with those natal factors.

This can help explain why a particular phase feels unusually active, restrictive, uncertain or transformative. The consultation can also identify areas that deserve attention during upcoming periods, while keeping timing within the larger context of the birth chart rather than treating a date as an isolated prediction.

WHAT WE EXPLORE
  • Mahadasha and Antardasha
  • Planetary condition in the natal chart
  • Transit influences
  • Current phase and its themes
  • Upcoming periods and transitions
BEFORE YOUR READING

Accurate birth time is particularly important for timing work. If your birth time has been rectified or is uncertain, mention that before the reading.
