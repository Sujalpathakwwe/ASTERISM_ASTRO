Who We Are

Astrology and divination, treated as a language of reflection.

Asterism Astro is a consultation and guidance practice built around astrology, palmistry and related divinatory traditions. We work with people who are curious about themselves, their relationships, their direction in life, or a period of change they are trying to understand.

Our approach is neither to dismiss these traditions as meaningless nor to present them as unquestionable truth. We believe there is value in studying an ancient system carefully while still leaving room for uncertainty, personal agency and the complexity of real life.

That balance is at the heart of Asterism Astro. A chart, a palm or a symbolic system can provide a framework for asking better questions. The purpose of a consultation is to explore that framework with you — not to take your decisions away from you.

Our Approach

Depth without unnecessary certainty.

We look for patterns, timing, context and connections rather than reducing a reading to a list of generic statements.

What We Believe

A reading should leave you with more perspective, not less agency.

We believe the most useful consultations are thoughtful, specific and grounded in the person's actual question.

Tradition with context

Ancient systems contain their own techniques, symbolism and internal logic. We take those systems seriously while recognising that they are being applied to people living very different lives from the societies in which many of these traditions developed.

Specific over generic

A consultation should be connected to the individual. We consider placements, patterns, timing and the circumstances behind a question instead of relying only on broad statements that could apply to anyone.

Curiosity over fear

Astrology and divination can easily be presented in a way that creates unnecessary fear. Our intention is to encourage understanding and reflection rather than make dramatic claims simply because they sound compelling.

Agency over fatalism

A difficult indication does not mean a person has no choices, and a favourable indication does not remove the need for effort. We treat interpretations as perspectives that can help someone think — not commands about what they must do.

The Consultation

It starts with your question.

A consultation is not simply a performance of a technique. Before interpretation begins, the question matters. What are you actually trying to understand? What has already happened? What feels uncertain? What part of the situation needs the most clarity?

Those details provide context. The same astrological placement or palm feature can be experienced differently by different people, and a useful reading needs to account for that rather than forcing every person into the same interpretation.

Depending on the service, you may be asked for birth information, palm photographs, handwriting or background details. The more relevant context you can provide, the more focused the consultation can become.

What We Are Not

We don't claim to know everything about your future.

There is a difference between offering an interpretation and claiming certainty. Asterism Astro does not promise that every prediction will happen exactly as described, nor do we believe a person should surrender their judgement to a reading.

Our consultations are not a replacement for medical care, mental-health support, legal advice or professional financial advice. When a question requires a qualified professional, that professional should remain part of the decision.

Our standard is simple

Be detailed where detail is useful. Be honest about uncertainty. Explain the reasoning behind an interpretation. Avoid unnecessary fear. And leave the final decision with the person whose life is being discussed.
