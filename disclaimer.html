1. General Nature of Our Services

Asterism Astro provides astrology, palmistry, graphology, tasseography, relationship analysis, and related traditional or interpretive services. These practices are offered as frameworks for reflection, personal exploration, and guidance. They should not be understood as objective guarantees about what will happen in a person's life.

2. Interpretations Are Not Certainties

A consultation may identify themes, tendencies, symbolic patterns, traditional indicators, or periods that are considered significant within a particular system. Such interpretations involve judgment and context. They cannot establish with certainty that a future event will occur or that one particular outcome is inevitable.

3. No Guaranteed Predictions

We do not guarantee predictions concerning relationships, marriage, employment, career changes, financial gains or losses, travel, family matters, health, or other life events. Circumstances change and individuals retain the ability to make different choices in response to the same circumstances.

6. Relationship and Personal Matters

7. Birth Information and Chart Accuracy

Astrological calculations can depend on the accuracy of the birth date, time, and place supplied by a client. Where the birth time is uncertain, some house placements and timing techniques may be less reliable. We may therefore identify uncertainty where the available birth information does not support a precise calculation.

8. Palmistry and Image Quality

Palmistry interpretations depend on the photographs or visual information supplied. Lighting, focus, camera angle, image resolution, skin condition, and the visibility of lines can affect what can reasonably be observed. A reading based on incomplete images may necessarily be limited.
