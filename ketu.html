THE SOUTH NODE

Ketu

✦

Detachment, Insight & Inner Awareness

In Vedic astrology, Ketu is traditionally associated with detachment, introspection, instinct and experiences that turn attention inward.

Ketu is a lunar node rather than a physical planet. Its symbolism is connected with familiarity, release, spiritual questioning and areas of life where worldly attachment may gradually lose its importance.

ASTROLOGICAL PERSPECTIVES

Understanding Ketu

01

Ketu in Vedic Astrology

In Vedic astrology, Ketu is the south lunar node and is traditionally associated with detachment, introspection, instinct and spiritual inquiry.

It represents areas where a person may already feel familiar or experienced, sometimes to the point where ordinary worldly satisfaction begins to feel insufficient.

Ketu's expression depends on its sign, house, conjunctions, aspects, dispositor and relationship with Rahu across the birth chart.

02

Ketu in Western Astrology

In Western astrology, the equivalent point is generally called the South Node. It is often interpreted as an area of familiarity, inherited patterns and tendencies that may already feel instinctive.

The South Node is commonly considered together with the North Node, representing the tension between familiar patterns and new developmental experiences.

BEYOND ASTROLOGY

Ketu in Other Traditions

✦

Ketu & Numerology

Some Indian numerological traditions associate Ketu with number 7. The symbolism is commonly connected with introspection, analysis, spirituality, research and inward awareness.

The emphasis is generally on depth rather than external display, with an interest in discovering what lies beneath the obvious.

Numerology and astrology are separate interpretive systems, and correspondences vary between traditions.
✦

Ketu in Palmistry

Ketu does not have a universally standardised physical representation in classical palmistry equivalent to the traditional planetary mounts.

Some modern Indian palmistry approaches use Ketu-related symbolism when discussing unusual markings, detachment, spiritual inclination or transformative patterns.

Palmistry traditions differ significantly, so these interpretations are not universal rules.

TRADITIONAL GUIDANCE

Ketu Remedies & Guidance

Traditional Ketu-oriented guidance focuses on developing awareness without turning detachment into isolation. The aim is to release unnecessary attachment while remaining engaged with life.

Maintain grounding routines Practise meditation and reflection Avoid unnecessary isolation Keep meaningful relationships active Reduce attachment to outcomes Serve others without expectation Study spiritual or philosophical traditions Make decisions with clarity rather than withdrawal
These are traditional cultural and spiritual practices, not scientifically established treatments.
THE LESSON OF KETU

Detachment is not emptiness. It is learning what no longer needs to control you.

Ketu represents the quiet process of release. Its deeper lesson is to carry forward the wisdom of experience without remaining trapped by attachment to what has already been learned.
